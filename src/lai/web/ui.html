A desktop it can actually use.

Say what you want done. LAI opens the real applications on this machine, reads their interfaces, clicks their buttons — and checks the result before telling you it is finished. It works on a screen of its own, so you can carry on with yours; when a task ends, the pages and files it produced open on your desktop.

What it has learned here

LAI writes these after a run that taught it something about this machine, and reads them back on the next one. They are plain markdown in ~/.lai/notes — correct anything that is wrong.
